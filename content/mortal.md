# Mortal

The **13+** addendum, one per anatomy × location and damage-type agnostic — past row 12 the
wound has stopped being characterised by what made it and is simply killing them.

Read **on top of** row 12, never instead of it, and always alongside the Fort save
(DC = the Critical Power total) the 13+ clamp already carries. So the Mechanic column is the
*extra* — what this body part does to someone past saving, not a restatement of row 12.

**Status:** draft

| Body part            | Effect            | Mechanic                                                                                                                                                                     |
|----------------------|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Humanoid · Arm       | Arm Torn Away     | The arm is severed at the shoulder. 2d6 bleed that a DC 20 Heal check or any magical healing stops; the limb is gone short of regeneration. On a failed save you die of shock rather than of the wound. |
| Humanoid · Leg       | Leg Torn Away     | The leg is severed at the hip. Prone, speed 0, and 2d6 bleed that a DC 20 Heal check or any magical healing stops; the limb is gone short of regeneration. On a failed save you die of shock rather than of the wound. |
| Humanoid · Torso     | Opened Up         | Torn open from sternum to hip. 2d6 bleed that a Heal check cannot stop — only magical healing closes it — and you cannot be stabilized while it bleeds. On a failed save you die at once. |
| Humanoid · Head      | Caved Cranium     | The skull gives way. **No save** — this is instant death. (Alternative if you'd rather keep the save universal: failure is instant death, success leaves you unconscious, dying, and permanently drained 1d6 Int/Wis/Cha.) |
| Beast · Arm          | Foreleg Torn Away | The foreleg is torn off at the shoulder. Prone, speed reduced to a quarter, and 2d6 bleed stopped by a DC 20 Heal check or magical healing. Natural attacks with that limb are lost. |
| Beast · Leg          | Hindleg Torn Away | The leg is torn off at the haunch. Prone, speed reduced to a quarter, and 2d6 bleed stopped by a DC 20 Heal check or magical healing. Cannot charge or run.                    |
| Beast · Torso        | Gutted            | The belly is opened and the viscera spill. 2d6 bleed that a Heal check cannot stop, and you cannot be stabilized while it bleeds. On a failed save you die at once.            |
| Beast · Head         | Skull Crushed     | **No save** — this is instant death. A creature whose head is not its seat of life (see the GM note below) takes the Gutted result instead.                                    |
| Beast · Tail         | Tail Torn Away    | The tail is severed at the base. 1d6 bleed stopped by a DC 15 Heal check or magical healing; permanently lose any tail attack, tail-based ability, and 2 points of Dex until regenerated. A failed save means shock, not death: unconscious and dying. |
| Beast · Wing         | Wing Torn Away    | The wing is torn away at the joint. Flight is lost outright; if airborne, you fall. 2d6 bleed stopped by a DC 20 Heal check or magical healing. A failed save kills on impact if airborne, otherwise leaves you dying. |
| Aberrant · Appendage | Appendage Severed | The limb is cut away entirely. 2d6 bleed (or the creature's equivalent) stopped by a DC 20 Heal check or magical healing; every attack and ability that used it is lost until regenerated. A failed save leaves you unconscious and dying. |
| Aberrant · Torso     | Ruptured          | The body cavity is breached and its contents void. 2d6 bleed that a Heal check cannot stop, and you cannot be stabilized while it bleeds. On a failed save you die at once.    |
| Aberrant · Head      | Cerebrum Destroyed | **No save** — this is instant death for a creature whose head holds its brain. One that has no such organ (an ooze, most plants) takes the Ruptured result instead.           |

## GM note — "seat of life"

Three of the thirteen kill outright rather than offering the Fort save, and all three are head
results, because a mortal wound to the head is the one place where "you get a save" stops reading
as a wound and starts reading as a miss.

That only works while the head is where the creature lives. Undead whose animus is elsewhere,
oozes and most plants (which take the aberrant table and have no cerebrum), swarms, and anything
with all-around vision and no cranium do not qualify — those take the torso result for their
anatomy instead. It is a judgement call and it is meant to be: the alternative is enumerating
every creature that keeps its brain somewhere unusual.

The three no-save rows are the one place these tables step outside the "13+ = Fort save DC = total
or die" rule from the concept doc. If you'd rather they didn't, the humanoid head row carries the
save-preserving alternative inline and the other two follow the same pattern.
