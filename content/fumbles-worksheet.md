## Thrown: three placements to replace

Thrown is full, but these three entries carry no real consequence on it: the weapon was leaving the
hand anyway. Each has a `note` in the pool saying so. Swap each one's `thrown` tag for something
better when it turns up.

| Entry | Why it's weak on thrown |
|---|---|
| Weapon Dropped | the throw was a miss either way; they draw another |
| Flung Weapon | a thrown weapon landing somewhere random costs nothing |
| Weapon Broken | matters only for a magic or returning weapon |

Already rejected for thrown: Overthrow, Wild Throw (no consequence), Fumbled Draw (avoided by not
throwing next), Weapon Stuck (the weapon is already gone).
