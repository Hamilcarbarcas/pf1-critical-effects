## Effects

### Arrow / Arrow - Lethal

#### Heart Pierced

Shot through the heart, and you're to blame. Instant death.

### Arrow

#### Compound Fracture

Gain the Broken Arm condition and 1d6 bleed. Roll randomly to determine which arm is affected.

A -6 penalty is applied to all attacks or skills that make use of that arm.

A broken arm can be healed by making a DC 10 Heal check to set the bone, followed by 10 HP of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Eye Plucked

Lose an eye. Roll randomly to determine which eye is lost.

All skills requiring sight, as well as attack rolls and reflex saves are made with a -2 penalty.

All intimidate checks are made with advantage.

The effects remain until the character grows accustomed to the loss of an eye. A DC 20 Wisdom check can be made each day to attempt to normalize to their new state, removing the associated penalties.

#### Femoral Artery

Gain the Severed Artery condition and 2d8 bleed.

Bleed damage cannot be stopped while the severed artery condition persists.

The Severed Artery condition can be removed with a DC 20 Heal check (as a standard action) or receiving 5 total points of hit point healing.

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Horrific Facial Scar

Gain 1d4 bleed. If the attacker gives a witty soliloquy, gain the stunned condition for 1 round.All intimidate checks are made with advantage.Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Knocked Down

Knocked prone.

#### Nethers

Stunned for 1 round, and all allies of defender in line of sight lose their immediate/swift action for the round as they gasp in empathy.

#### Pierced Foot

Cannot run or charge, and skills requiring footwork are rolled with disadvantage for 1d4 days. Roll randomly to determine which foot is affected.

The effects of a pierced foot can be removed with 5 HP of dedicated healing to the pierced foot (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Pierced Hand

Any items in the affected hand are dropped, and any skills requiring the use of that hand are made with disadvantage for 1d4 days. Roll randomly to determine which hand is affected.

The effects of a pierced hand can be removed with 5 HP of dedicated healing to the pierced hand (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Pierced Heart

Immediately dropped to -1 HP and 1d12 bleed.

#### Pierced Knee

Gain the broken knee condition. Roll randomly to determine which leg is affected.

A -6 penalty is applied to AC, movement is reduced to 5 feet (or half normal with the use of crutches), you cannot run or charge, and any skills requiring footwork automatically fail.

A broken knee can be healed by making a DC 20 Heal check to set the bone, followed by 20 HP of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Severed Fingers

Lose 1d4 fingers, gain 1 stacking bleed for each lost finger, and drop all items held in that hand. Roll randomly to determine which hand is affected.All skills requiring the use of that hand are made with disadvantage. If 3 or more fingers are lost on a hand, that hand cannot be used to wield a weapon without the aid of a prosthesis.If the lost fingers are recovered, they can be reattached by making a DC 20 Heal check within 10 minutes of the loss, followed by 10 HP of healing dedicated to each finger lost (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).If the fingers are not recovered, the effects remain until the character grows accustomed to the loss of those fingers. A Wisdom check can be made each day to attempt to normalize to their new state. The base DC is 20, +1 for each finger tipped. This does not alleviate the need for a prosthesis.

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Throat Pierced

Gain the Pierced Throat condition and 1d8 bleed.

You cannot speak above a whisper.

You cannot cast spells with somatic components.

The pierced throat condition can be healed with 10 HP of healing dedicated to it (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

### Bludgeoning / Bludgeoning - Arms

#### Broken Arm

Gain the Broken Arm Condition. Roll randomly to determine which arm is broken.

A -6 penalty is applied to all attacks or skills that make use of that arm.

A broken arm can be healed by making a DC 10 Heal check to set the bone, followed by 10 HP of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Broken Elbow

Gain the Broken Elbow Condition. Roll randomly to determine which elbow is broken.

A -6 penalty is applied to all attacks or skills that make use of that arm.

A broken elbow can be healed by making a DC 15 Heal check to set the bone, followed by 15 HP of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Broken Finger

Gain the broken finger condition. Roll randomly to determine which hand is affected.

All skill checks using that hand are made with disadvantage.

If 2 or more fingers are broken on one hand, they are replaced with the broken hand condition.

A broken finger can be healed by making a DC 10 Heal check to set the bone, followed by 5 HP of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Broken Hand

*Effects*

Gain the broken hand condition. Roll randomly to determine which hand is affected.

A -4 penalty is applied to all attacks or skills that make use of that hand.

Whenever a broken hand is used in an attack or skill check, make a DC 10 Fortitude save. Failure turns the broken hand into a fractured hand.

A broken hand can be healed by making a DC 10 heal check to set the bone, followed by 10 points of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Broken Shoulder

Gain the Broken Shoulder condition. Roll randomly to determine which arm is affected.

The affected arm is useless, and any rolls involving its use automatically fail. Some work involving only hand movements (such as disable device) may ignore this penalty, at DM discretion.

A broken shoulder can be healed by making a DC 20 heal check, followed by 20 HP of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Compound Fracture

Gain the Broken Arm condition and 1d6 bleed. Roll randomly to determine which arm is affected.

A -6 penalty is applied to all attacks or skills that make use of that arm.

A broken arm can be healed by making a DC 10 Heal check to set the bone, followed by 10 HP of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Dropped Item

Any item(s) held in the hand of the attacker's choice are dropped.

#### Dropped Weapon

Any item(s) held in the hand of the attacker's choice are dropped.

#### Flung Weapon

Weapon is thrown 1d6x5 feet in a random direction.

#### Shattered Hand

*Effects*

Gain the shattered hand condition. Roll randomly to determine which wrist is affected.

The affected hand is useless, and any rolls involving its use automatically fail.

A shattered hand can be healed by making a DC 20 heal check to set the bone, followed by 20 points of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Shattered Wrist

*Effects*

Gain the shattered wrist condition. Roll randomly to determine which wrist is affected.

The affected hand is useless, and any rolls involving its use automatically fail.

A shattered wrist can be healed by making a DC 25 heal check to set the bone, followed by 25 points of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

### Bludgeoning / Bludgeoning - Head

#### Broken Jaw

Gain the Broken Jaw condition.

All skills requiring speech are made with disadvantage.

When casting spells with somatic components, you must make a concentration check (DC 15 + double the level of the spell you’re casting) to succeed. You lose the spell if you fail.

A broken jaw can be healed by making a DC 10 Heal check to set the bone, followed by 15 HP of healing dedicated to the break (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Broken Neck

You cannot speak above a whisper.You cannot cast spells with somatic components.

A crushed larynx be healed with 20 HP of healing dedicated to it (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

#### Dazing Blow

Gain the Stunned condition for 1 round.

#### Dropped Item

Any item(s) held in the hand of the attacker's choice are dropped.

#### Kidney Strike

Stunned for 1 round and fatigued.

#### Knocked Down

Knocked prone.

#### Knocked Out

Gain the unconscious condition for 1d4 rounds.

At the end of each of your turns, you may make a DC 15 Fortitude save. On success, regain consciousness.

#### Lost Teeth

Attacker may make one attack of opportunity against the target.

All diplomacy checks are made with disadvantage.

False teeth may reduce or remove this effect.

#### Off Balance

Knocked prone. Victim may spend an immediate action to remain standing. If they do so, they are flat footed and have a movement of 5 feet until the end of their next turn.

#### Wind Knocked Out

Stunned for 1d4 rounds.

You may roll a DC 12 Fortitude save at the end of each of your turns to end the stunned effect early.

### Vorpal

#### Beheaded

Instant death.

#### Disemboweled

Gain the Disemboweled condition and 1d12 bleed.

Staggered until cured.

A disembowel can be cured by pushing the innards back into the body as a standard action (requires a DC 15 Fortitude save to do it yourself), followed by a DC 20 heal check or 10 points of dedicated healing to seal the wound (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Finger Tipped

Lose 1d4 fingertips and gain 1 bleed. Roll randomly to determine which hand is affected.

All skills requiring the use of that hand are made with disadvantage.

If the fingertips are recovered, they can be reattached by making a DC 15 Heal check within 10 minutes of the loss, followed by 5 HP of healing dedicated to each finger tipped (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

If the fingertips are not recovered, the effects remain until the character grows accustomed to the loss of those tips. A Wisdom check can be made each day to attempt to normalize to their new state. The base DC is 15, +1 for each finger tipped.

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Lost Fingers

Lose 1d4 fingers, gain 1 stacking bleed for each lost finger, and drop all items held in that hand. Roll randomly to determine which hand is affected.All skills requiring the use of that hand are made with disadvantage. If 3 or more fingers are lost on a hand, that hand cannot be used to wield a weapon without the aid of a prosthesis.If the lost fingers are recovered, they can be reattached by making a DC 20 Heal check within 10 minutes of the loss, followed by 10 HP of healing dedicated to each finger lost (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).If the fingers are not recovered, the effects remain until the character grows accustomed to the loss of those fingers. A Wisdom check can be made each day to attempt to normalize to their new state. The base DC is 20, +1 for each finger tipped. This does not alleviate the need for a prosthesis.

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Lost Toes

Movement rates are halved.

If the toes are recovered, they can be reattached by making a DC 15 Heal check within 10 minutes of the loss, followed by 5 HP of healing dedicated to each toe lost (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

If the toes are not recovered, the effects remain until the character grows accustomed to the loss of those toes. A Wisdom check can be made each day to attempt to normalize to their new state. The base DC is 10, +1 for each toe lost.

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Scalped

Gain the Stunned condition for 1 round and 1d10 bleed.

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Severed Arm

Lose an arm and gain 1d10 bleed. Roll randomly to see which arm is affected.

A severed arm is cut somewhere between the elbow and shoulder.

If the arm can be recovered, it can be reattached by making a DC 25 Heal check within 10 minutes of the loss, followed by 40 HP of healing dedicated to the arm (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

Partial to total use of a lost arm can be regained via the use of a prosthesis.

#### Severed Ear

Gain 1d4 bleed.

All checks requiring hearing are made with disadvantage.

If the ear can be recovered, it can be reattached by making a DC 20 Heal check within 10 minutes of the loss, followed by 15 HP of healing dedicated to the ear (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

The effect remains until the character grows accustomed to the loss of the ear. A DC 20 Wisdom check can be made each day to attempt to normalize to their new state, removing the associated penalties.

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

#### Severed Foot

Lose a foot and gain 1d6 bleed. Roll randomly to see which foot is affected.

A -4 penalty is applied to AC, movement is reduced to 5 feet (or half normal with the use of crutches), you cannot run or charge, and any skills requiring footwork automatically fail.

A severed foot is cut somewhere between the ankle and knee.

If the foot can be recovered, it can be reattached by making a DC 25 Heal check within 10 minutes of the loss, followed by 35 HP of healing dedicated to the foot (dedicated healing does not heal hit point damage).

Bleed damage is applied at the beginning of each turn until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or receiving 1 or more points of hit point healing.

Partial to total use of a lost foot can be regained via the use of a prosthesis.
